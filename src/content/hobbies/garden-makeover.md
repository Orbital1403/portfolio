I grew up as a **city kid in an apartment block** — the closest I ever got to grass was the patch around the playground or the dusty football pitches

Fast-forward a couple of decades and here I am, figuring out _aeration, verticutting, topdressing, overseeding, fertilizing_ — all from scratch.

Sure, I’d _smoked grass_ before — but never **Astro grass** 🌱😂 (proper _Only Fools and Horses_ material).

Every year brings a new phase: clearing soil, fixing drains, waterproofing patios, setting up solar lights… and learning the hard way that grass doesn’t magically grow just because you shout at it.

This makeover isn’t just about a lawn; it’s me turning from a clueless rookie into a weekend gardener — one muddy step at a time.
