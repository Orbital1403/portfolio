<h2 id="a-practical-mind-a-curious-nature"><TopicIcon name="about" heading />A practical mind, a curious nature</h2>

I'm **Gordan Grgić**, originally from Zagreb. I've been living in Brussels for some time now. My technical experience includes work in industrial electronics, quality control, calibration and fault analysis, alongside repairs and renovation. I have always enjoyed understanding a problem and finding a solution that works.

<h2 id="it-started-with-computers"><TopicIcon name="firmware" heading />It started with computers</h2>

I have been around computers since primary school, starting with a Commodore 64 and moving through PC generations from the XT onward. As a child, I was already putting computers together for friends. Finding the faulty component or noticing something others had missed was part of the attraction.

I still use **Linux, macOS and Windows**. My interest in technology has stayed practical: what can it do, how does it work, and how can I make it suit the task?

<h2 id="experience-in-electronics"><TopicIcon name="bench-meter" heading />Experience in electronics</h2>

At **Siemens (October 2006 – December 2011)**, I started in the production of pressure and temperature transmitters before moving into quality control. I analysed returned devices and carefully documented my findings. Those reports later contributed to improvements within the department.

At **Altpro in Zagreb (November 2013 – December 2017)**, I worked in quality control and calibration for railway safety systems, in a department closely connected to development. It gave me further experience with measurement, testing and the details that affect whether a device works as intended.

<h2 id="a-new-chapter-in-brussels"><TopicIcon name="location" heading />A new chapter in Brussels</h2>

Moving to Belgium meant starting again: discovering the city, the country and a different way of life. I have done independent technical work in Brussels since 2022.

I repaired household appliances, worked on installations, painted and fitted joinery, and helped renovate apartments. Much of it was new to me, so I learned through doing.

<h2 id="music-brought-the-pieces-together"><TopicIcon name="audio" heading />Music brought the pieces together</h2>

Music has always mattered to me. I explore it, travel for it and enjoy the equipment that brings it into a room.

That interest led to **[MILA\*](/projects/mila/)**, my personal audio streamer. I had the components at home and a clear idea of what frustrated me about existing players. I put my requirements on paper and began developing an interface around my own listening habits.

I lead the project from requirements to release: I decide what it should do, shape its behaviour, test every change on the real hardware and document what I find. It is still in development, and I am learning as it grows.

<h2 id="where-i-want-to-go"><TopicIcon name="education" heading />Continued learning</h2>

After completing my Java studies, I enrolled in the **AI & Machine Learning programme at ITAcademy**, which I am currently attending. The programme covers Python, data processing, machine learning, deep learning and deploying AI/ML systems. Alongside these studies, MILA\* gives me a practical project through which to deepen my understanding of Python.

Away from projects, I enjoy music, cycling and finding out how things work.
