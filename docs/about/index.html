PERSONAL FIELD NOTES / MY STORY

About me

A practical mind, a curious nature

I’m Gordan Grgić, originally from Zagreb and now based in Brussels. My work has taken me through electronics, quality control, repairs and renovation. Across those different settings, I have always enjoyed understanding a problem and finding a workable solution.

It started with computers

I have been around computers since primary school, starting with a Commodore 64 and moving through PC generations from the XT onward. As a child, I was already putting computers together for friends. Finding the faulty component or noticing something others had missed was part of the attraction.

I still use Linux, macOS and Windows. My interest in technology has stayed practical: what can it do, how does it work, and how can I make it suit the task?

Experience in electronics

At Siemens, I started in the production of pressure and temperature transmitters before moving into quality control. I analysed returned devices and carefully documented my findings. Those reports later contributed to improvements within the department.

At Altpro in Zagreb, I worked in quality control and calibration for railway safety systems, in a department closely connected to development. It gave me further experience with measurement, testing and the details that affect whether a device works as intended.

A new chapter in Brussels

Moving to Belgium meant starting again: discovering the city, the country and a different way of life. Through people I knew, practical repair work gradually became a large part of my working life.

I repaired household appliances, worked on installations, painted and fitted joinery, and helped renovate apartments. Much of it was new to me, so I learned through doing. That experience broadened my skills; I now want to bring them into a more focused technical role.

Music brought the pieces together

Music has always mattered to me. I explore it, travel for it and enjoy the equipment that brings it into a room.

That interest led to MILA*, my personal audio streamer. I had the components at home and a clear idea of what frustrated me about existing players. I put my requirements on paper and began developing an interface around my own listening habits.

I lead the project from requirements to release: I decide what it should do, shape its behaviour, test every change on the real hardware and document what I find. AI coding assistants write much of the code under that direction. It is still in development, and I am learning as it grows.

Where I want to go

I am interested in work that combines practical technical knowledge with investigation and attention to detail: testing, diagnostics, technical support or integration, especially where hardware meets software.

I have completed my Java studies and am now turning my attention to Python. MILA*, whose backend is written in Python, gives me a practical project through which to explore it.

Away from projects, I enjoy music, cycling and finding out how things work.
