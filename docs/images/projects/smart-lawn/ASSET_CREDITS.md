# Smart Lawn image sources

The lawn photograph was supplied by Gordan Grgić in the Smart Lawn planning conversation. Its publication copy has been resized and stripped of camera metadata.

The component photographs are reference images from the Espressif, DFRobot and Adafruit product pages, and RS Components (Mean Well RS-series housing reference supplied by the user) linked in `sources.json`. These legacy reference assets are retained from the earlier concept gallery. They illustrate proposed component types; they are not photographs of an assembled Smart Lawn system or a final bill of materials.

Third-party photographs remain the property of their respective owners. The repository's MIT licence does not apply to these photographs.

The power photograph shows the RS-series enclosure, using an RS-150-24 reference photo. The concept specifies a 12 V variant; no final model has been selected.

## Hardware reference photographs — 29 October 2026

The current gallery uses photographs of the same component types from the sources below. They are illustrative references, not claims that these brands or exact models were ordered or tested. Models, dimensions, appearance and pictured quantities may differ from the selected equipment.

- Controller: Waveshare ESP32-S3-RS485-CAN reference model.
- Soil probes: Renke temperature/moisture and integrated soil-sensor product pages. The integrated family includes a temperature/moisture/pH variant and an EC variant; the project continues to treat pH as experimental.
- Enclosure and DC screw-terminal plug: Adafruit product photographs. The pictured clear-lid enclosure differs from the ordered box.
- DIN rail: Solar DIY product photograph.
- Four-core internal wire: LEDsales product photograph. The reference is flat silicone hookup wire; the ordered cable has a round outer jacket.
- Inline glass-fuse holders: Tian Rui product photograph. A pictured rating is not the selected or validated fuse rating for the assembly.
- PG7 cable gland: DFRobot product photograph.

Exact product-page and image URLs are recorded under `reference-hardware-gallery` in `sources.json`, with source links displayed beneath each photograph. The previous small screenshot crops have been replaced. No order screenshots or private purchase information are published.

Product photographs and branding remain the property of their respective owners and are excluded from the repository's MIT licence. Images do not validate supplier performance or protection-rating claims.

## Additional ordered components — 30 October 2026

The hardware overview now includes the newly confirmed cable, power supply, UK2.5B terminal-block kit and 120 Ω / 0.5 W termination resistors. The additional photographs illustrate component types, not the delivered or assembled equipment:

- `hardware/field-cable-reference.jpg`: Onninen catalogue photograph for BITNER LP0170, showing both blue and green BiTsensor cable-family variants. The selected cable is LP0170; neither pictured quantity nor the green variant is a purchase claim.
- `hardware/power-supply-reference.jpg`: Adafruit product 352, an enclosed 12 V / 5 A adapter with a US mains plug. The purchased supply's exact model, plug, rating and protection class are not established by this photograph. The image is a form-factor reference only.
- `hardware/terminal-blocks-reference.webp`: AOSI UK2.5B terminal-block photograph. It shows one component, not the full purchased kit or a verified supplier match.

Exact source links are stored under `reference-hardware-gallery` in `sources.json` and displayed in the gallery. Cable dimensions are supported by the BITNER technical sheet linked there. No order IDs, prices or private purchase screenshots are included.
