# Smart Lawn image sources

The lawn photograph was supplied by Gordan Grgić in the Smart Lawn planning conversation. Its publication copy has been resized and stripped of camera metadata.

The component photographs are reference images from the Espressif, DFRobot and Adafruit product pages, and RS Components (Mean Well RS-series housing reference supplied by the user) linked in `sources.json` and on the Smart Lawn page. They illustrate proposed component types; they are not photographs of an assembled Smart Lawn system or a final bill of materials.

Third-party photographs remain the property of their respective owners. The repository's MIT licence does not apply to these photographs.

The power photograph shows the RS-series enclosure, using an RS-150-24 reference photo. The concept specifies a 12 V variant; no final model has been selected.

## My Garden software prototype — 29 October 2026

`my-garden-dashboard.jpg` is a screenshot of Gordan Grgić's My Garden demo interface. Readings are simulated and the root-zone illustration is illustrative. The self-contained demo is published at `/demos/my-garden/`, with cloud configuration disabled. It represents a local software milestone, not an installed or calibrated sensor system.
